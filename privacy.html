Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026.

We collect data to improve the user experience. The game has no advertising and no account of its own.

Results

The game keeps your ten best results with their dates on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, it also keeps them there for your other Apple TV devices.

Game Center

When you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends the score of a round that you end or lose to the leaderboard. If you sign in later, it can send your best score since the launch. Apple shows it with your Game Center name and keeps it under its own privacy policy.
